Gender Wage Gap Visualization

Welcome to this interactive visualization exploring the gender wage gap across different demographics and time periods. This visualization uses scrollytelling to guide you through various insights about wage disparities.

Navigation Instructions

  • Arrow Buttons: Use the arrow buttons at the bottom of the page to navigate through different visualizations.
  • Mouse Wheel: You can also scroll with your mouse wheel to move between visualizations.
  • Hover: Hover over data points to see detailed information.
  • Zoom Controls: Use the controls in the top right of the visualization to filter and zoom data (available for bar charts and box plots).

Types of Visualizations

  • Line Chart: Shows the trend of men's and women's wages over time, with the percentage gap.
  • Bar Charts: Compare wages across different categories like age groups, occupations, and education levels.
  • Stacked Bar Chart: Shows the proportion of men and women in different occupations.
  • Box Plot: Displays the distribution of hours worked by full-time and part-time workers by gender.

Filtering Data

Each visualization (except the line chart) has specific filter controls in the top right panel:

  • Category Filter: For bar charts, select which categories to display.
  • Employment Type Filter: For box plots, filter by employment type.
